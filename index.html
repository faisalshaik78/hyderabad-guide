Landmarks

Old City

Charminar

The 16th-century monument with four minarets, surrounded by the busy Laad Bazaar for bangles and pearls.

Fort

Golconda Fort

A hilltop fortress known for its acoustics and for the evening sound-and-light show.

Lake

Hussain Sagar

A heart-shaped lake with a giant Buddha statue and a promenade for evening walks.

Food & Drink

  • Hyderabadi biryaniDum-cooked rice and meat layered with saffron and spices.
  • HaleemA slow-cooked wheat and meat stew, especially popular during Ramadan.
  • Irani chaiStrong, milky tea, usually paired with Osmania biscuits.
  • Double ka meethaA rich bread pudding dessert with dry fruits.

Getting Around

Metro Rail

Fast and air-conditioned. The best choice for crossing the city during peak hours.

City buses

An extensive network that covers areas the Metro doesn't reach.

Autos & cabs

Available through ride-hailing apps for short trips and late-night travel.
